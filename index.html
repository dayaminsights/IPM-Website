Since 1974

A Heritage of Distinction

What began fifty years ago as a single brass workshop in Patparganj, Delhi, has matured into one of India's most trusted names in bath fittings.

Every valve, spout and mixer is designed, machined and finished under one roof — solid brass, never plated zinc, in sixteen hand-perfected finishes. You will find creativity and meticulous craftsmanship throughout the collection.
