Four Generations, One Vision

Est. 1966  ·  Delhi & NCR
196601

The Founding Vision

Great-Grandfather · Founder

Driven by a deep commitment to improving hygiene standards across India, our great-grandfather established IPM with a singular belief: every home deserves access to quality bathroom fittings. The foundation was laid not just in brass and steel, but in values that endure to this day.

1980s02

Expanding the Legacy

Grandfather · Growth Era

Our grandfather expanded both the product range and the reach of IPM, ensuring that durable brass fittings became accessible not just to city dwellers, but to households across India. Reliability and longevity became the hallmarks of our craft during this era.

2000s03

Modern Manufacturing

Father · Modernisation

Embracing cutting-edge technology, our father transformed IPM's manufacturing capabilities. New automated production lines, quality control systems, and an expanded dealer network brought the brand into the modern era — without ever compromising the hand-crafted precision at our core.

Today04

Global Ambitions

Abhishek Kapoor · Director

Under Abhishek Kapoor's leadership, IPM is stepping onto the world stage. With a presence in India and the UAE, and a commitment to the Make in India initiative, we are championing Indian craftsmanship globally — proving that luxury and conscience can coexist beautifully.
