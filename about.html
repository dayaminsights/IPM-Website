Sustainability First

Every Drop
Matters

India faces a water crisis that demands innovation at every level. At IPM, we engineer our brass faucets to flow at as little as 4 litres per minute—compared to the standard plastic tap's 26 litres per minute. That's a saving of approximately 85% per use.

Multiply that across millions of homes, and the impact is profound. Quality bathroom fittings are not just a luxury—they are an act of environmental stewardship.
